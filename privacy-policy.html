HoneyTide Apiary Privacy Policy

HoneyTide Apiary respects your privacy. This policy explains what limited information may be collected when you visit this website or contact HoneyTide Apiary directly.

Last updated: July 8, 2026.

Website Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand general website traffic, page visits, device types, and how visitors find the site. This helps HoneyTide understand which pages are useful and where the website can be improved.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure website activity. HoneyTide Apiary does not use Google Analytics to personally identify individual visitors.

Cookies and Local Storage

HoneyTide Apiary does not currently set its own cookies, create user accounts, or store visitor information in local storage. Third-party services, including Google Analytics, may use cookies or similar browser technologies as part of their service.

Contact Email

If you email HoneyTide Apiary, your email address and the information you choose to include may be used to reply to your message, answer questions, provide swarm guidance, or follow up about HoneyTide-related topics.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information by email unless it is necessary for your request.

No Sale of Personal Information

HoneyTide Apiary does not sell personal information. Contact information shared by email is not sold, rented, or traded.

External Links

This website links to outside resources such as Facebook, YouTube, Oregon State Beekeepers Association, OSU Extension, and Oregon Bee Project. Those websites have their own privacy practices and policies.

Questions

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how HoneyTide Apiary handles contact information, email honeytideapiary@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated as HoneyTide Apiary's website, services, or tools change.
